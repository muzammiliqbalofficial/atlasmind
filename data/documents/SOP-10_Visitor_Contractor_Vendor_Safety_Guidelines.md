## 1. Scope & Security Objective
To protect personnel, manufacturing equipment, proprietary production techniques, and intellectual property, Atlas Honda maintains strict perimeter security and occupational safety standards for all non-permanent personnel entering the Karachi Mother Plant.

This guideline applies to short-term business visitors, technical consultants, external construction/maintenance contractors, equipment vendors, and logistics haulers.

---

## 2. Gate Entry & Identification Protocols

### 2.1 Gate Classification
- **Gate 1 (Executive & Official Visitors):** Reserved for customers, corporate clients, auditors, government officials, and official business delegations.
- **Gate 2 (Vendors, Contractors & Freight):** Designated for contractor workforces, heavy transport trailers carrying motorcycle parts/raw steel coils, and equipment delivery trucks.

### 2.2 Security Clearance Steps
1. **Identification Deposit:** All visitors and contractor personnel must present and deposit an original valid National Identity Card (CNIC), Passport, or Driving License at the Security Reception.
2. **Visitor Pass Issuance:** A numbered, color-coded RFID badge is issued:
   - *Red Badge:* External Contractor / Maintenance (valid for authorized work zones only).
   - *Blue Badge:* Official Business Visitor (requires continuous AHL staff escort).
   - *Green Badge:* VIP / Japanese Technical Delegation.
3. **Safety Briefing Video:** Prior to crossing into production bays, first-time visitors must view a mandatory **5-minute Safety Induction Video** at the Gate Reception covering emergency alarm signals, escape routes, and PPE rules.

## 3. Contractor Safety & Permit-to-Work (PTW) System

No contractor or external technician may perform any non-routine, installation, maintenance, or civil construction task without obtaining an approved **Permit to Work (PTW)** from the HSE Department.

### 3.1 Specialized Permit Categories
- **Hot Work Permit:** Mandatory for gas cutting, electric arc welding, brazing, grinding, or open-flame operations. Requires:
  - 10-meter clearance of flammable materials (oil, thinners, packaging cartons).
  - Standby calibrated $6\text{ kg}$ ABC dry chemical powder fire extinguisher.
  - Dedicated fire-watch observer present throughout work and 30 minutes post-work.
- **Work at Height Permit:** Mandatory for any activity conducted at an elevation of **$2.0\text{ meters or higher}$** above ground. Requires:
  - Full-body safety harness with dual shock-absorbing lanyards anchored to certified structural points.
  - Inspected tubular steel scaffolding with toe-boards and guardrails (bamboo scaffolding strictly banned).
- **Confined Space Entry Permit:** Mandatory for entering paint sludge pits, underground drainage tanks, storm-water sumps, or diesel generator exhaust shafts. Requires:
  - Multi-gas atmospheric testing ($O_2 \ge 19.5\%$, $H_2S \le 10\text{ ppm}$, $CO \le 25\text{ ppm}$, LEL $< 5\%$).
  - Continuous forced-air ventilation and external standby safety watcher with lifeline.

### 3.2 Child Labor & Labor Standards Compliance
Contractors are strictly bound by Atlas Honda’s human rights policy. Any contractor engaging individuals under the age of 18 or failing to provide statutory minimum wages will face immediate contract cancellation, blacklisting, and referral to provincial labor authorities.

## 4. Visitor Conduct & Environmental Rules
- **Continuous Escort:** Visitors must remain with their designated Atlas Honda host employee at all times. Wandering unaccompanied through active robotic welding or press stamping shops is strictly prohibited.
- **Photography Prohibition:** Photography, video recording, and aerial drone recording on company premises are strictly prohibited without written authorization from the General Manager Plant. Camera lenses on smartphones of technical visitors may be sealed with tamper-evident stickers at Gate Reception.
- **Smoking & Tobacco Policy:** Atlas Honda is a smoke-free facility. Smoking (including e-cigarettes and vapes) and chewing pan/gutka are strictly banned, except in designated outdoor smoking gazebos.
- **Emergency Evacuation:** In the event of an emergency siren (intermittent loud pulsing horn):
  - Cease all work immediately.
  - Follow the green exit signs led by your host employee.
  - Assemble at **Assembly Point 1 (Main Lawn, Gate 1)** or **Assembly Point 2 (Sports Ground, West Perimeter)**.
  - Await roll-call headcount; do not re-enter buildings until the "All-Clear" continuous siren is sounded.
