## 1. Policy Purpose & Stance
Atlas Honda Limited (ATLH) enforces an uncompromised commitment to legal compliance, financial integrity, human rights, and environmental responsibility. Employees and business partners often detect unethical conduct before formal audits reveal them.

The Whistleblower / Speak-Up Policy provides a secure, confidential, and officially protected mechanism allowing any employee, contractor, vendor, or community member to report suspected illicit conduct without fear of retaliation, demotion, harassment, or detriment.

---

## 2. Qualifying Protected Disclosures
Reports made in good faith regarding any of the following matters constitute **Protected Disclosures**:
- Financial irregularities, accounting fraud, intentional falsification of financial ledgers, or SECP non-compliance.
- Bribery, illegal commissions, extortion, or kickbacks paid to/from vendors, public officials, or dealers.
- Intentional tampering with plant safety equipment, bypass of safety interlocks, or deliberate concealment of toxic emissions/hazardous waste disposal.
- Deliberate falsification of quality inspection records (e.g. bypassing brake or emissions testing on CD70/CG125 motorcycles).
- Harassment, discriminatory persecution, or gross abuse of supervisory authority.
- Compromising confidential intellectual property, design CAD files, or commercial secrets.

## 4. Whistleblower Committee Composition & Governance
The Whistleblower Review Committee oversees all incoming disclosures and is composed of:
- **Secretary:** Head of Internal Audit
- **Member:** Head of Legal & Corporate Affairs
- **Member:** General Manager Human Resources  
*(If an allegation involves any committee member, that member is recused immediately, and oversight transfers exclusively to the Chairman of the Board Audit Committee).*

## 5. Investigation Workflow & Timelines

| Stage | Action & Mandate | Statutory Timeline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Acknowledgment** | Secure receipt acknowledgment sent to the whistleblower (if contact channel provided). | Within 48 Hours |
| **Stage 2: Preliminary Screening** | Committee assesses prima facie credibility; determines whether full forensic or operational investigation is warranted. | Within 5 Working Days |
| **Stage 3: Comprehensive Investigation** | Independent audit or technical investigation conducted; evidence gathered; implicated parties interviewed under strict due process. | Completed within 30 Working Days |
| **Stage 4: Executive Findings Report** | Formal investigation dossier submitted to CEO and Chairman of Board Audit Committee with remedial recommendations. | Within 10 Days of Inquiry Completion |
| **Stage 5: Remedial Action & Oversight** | Implementation of corrective action (disciplinary dismissals, vendor debarment, legal restitution, police reporting). | Monitored until full resolution |

## 6. Strict Non-Retaliation Guarantee & Legal Immunity
- **Zero Retaliation Guarantee:** Atlas Honda strictly prohibits retaliation of any form (including adverse performance appraisals, termination, demotion, transfer to undesirable shifts, or informal isolation) against any person who raises a concern in good faith.
- **Punishment for Retaliation:** Any employee found to have retaliated against a whistleblower will be subject to immediate summary dismissal under **Gross Misconduct**.
- **Malicious Allegations:** If an inquiry establishes beyond doubt that an allegation was fabricated knowingly and maliciously to defame a coworker, the complainant will face disciplinary action. Good-faith reports that prove uncorroborated will never attract any adverse consequence.
