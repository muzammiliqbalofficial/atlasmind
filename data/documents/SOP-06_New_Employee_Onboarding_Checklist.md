## 1. Overview & Onboarding Philosophy
Atlas Honda adheres to the guiding principle of **"Organizational Development through Self Development"**. A structured, seamless onboarding journey transforms newly hired professionals and technical operators into disciplined, safety-conscious, and performance-oriented members of the Atlas family.

This Standard Operating Procedure delineates the sequential milestones from Pre-Arrival (Day 0) to Confirmation (Day 180).

---

## 2. Onboarding Timeline & Checkpoints

### Phase 1: Pre-Arrival & Day 0 (HR Operations & IT)
- [ ] **Document Verification:** Verification of original educational degrees (HEC attestation for officers), CNIC verification via NADRA Verisys, prior employment experience certificates, and police character verification.
- [ ] **Pre-Employment Medical Checkup:** Medical fitness certification conducted at Atlas Honda Karachi Plant Clinic (F-36 S.I.T.E.) or designated diagnostic laboratory.
- [ ] **System Provisioning:** HR initiates SAP HR personnel number generation, biometrics profile creation, issuing company email address (`@atlashonda.com.pk`), and hardware assignment requisition.

### Phase 2: Day 1 - Welcoming & Core Induction
- [ ] **HR Reception:** Arrival at Main Gate 1 Security Reception; issuance of temporary visitor pass and escort to HR Training Center.
- [ ] **Welcome Kit Handover:** Employee Handbook, printed Code of Conduct, Atlas Group corporate diary, and formal appointment letter execution.
- [ ] **Smart ID Card & Biometric Enrollment:** Capture of facial recognition data and generation of magnetic RFID smart card for turnstile access and subsidized cafeteria dining.
- [ ] **Safety PPE Issuance:** Issuance of personal safety shoes (steel-toe), safety goggles, bump cap/hard hat, and plant uniform sets.
- [ ] **Facility Tour:** Guided tour of Karachi Plant operations, including Press Shop, Welding, Paint Line, Engine Assembly, Main Assembly Conveyor, Quality Roller Booth, and Central Cafeteria.

### Phase 3: Week 1 - Foundational Training & Cultural Integration
- [ ] **HSE Safety Induction (Mandatory):** 4-hour certified session on fire prevention, ISO 14001/45001 standards, emergency evacuation assembly points, and shop floor hazard rules.
- [ ] **The Honda Philosophy & 5S Training:** Introduction to the Three Joys (The Joy of Buying, The Joy of Selling, The Joy of Creating), Kaizen continuous improvement, and the Japanese 5S methodology:
  1. *Seiri* (Sort / چھانٹیں)
  2. *Seiton* (Set in Order / ترتیب دیں)
  3. *Seiso* (Shine / صفائی رکھیں)
  4. *Seiketsu* (Standardize / معیار بنائیں)
  5. *Shitsuke* (Sustain / پابندی کریں)
- [ ] **Buddy / Mentor Assignment:** Assignment of an experienced departmental peer (Buddy) to guide the new recruit through day-to-day administrative and workflow routines.

### Phase 4: Month 1 (Day 30 Review)
- [ ] **KPI Alignment Session:** One-on-one dialogue between new hire and Line Manager to define Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and 6-month probationary targets.
- [ ] **HR Touchpoint:** Informal 30-minute check-in with HR Business Partner to address any relocation, transportation, or peer adjustment difficulties.

### Phase 5: Month 3 (Day 90 Mid-Probation Evaluation)
- [ ] **Performance Progress Review:** Formal appraisal form completed by Line Manager evaluating technical competence, safety adherence, attendance punctuality, and cultural fit.
- [ ] **Remedial Action (if required):** Identification of specific skill gaps and scheduling of targeted technical training at the Atlas Honda Technical Training Center (TTC).

### Phase 6: Month 6 (Day 180 Confirmation Appraisal)
- [ ] **Probation Appraisal Dossier:** Formal review meeting convened with Department Head and GM Human Resources.
- [ ] **Outcome Determination:**
  - Option A: Satisfactory performance leads to official Letter of Employment Confirmation.
  - Option B: Conditional probation extension (maximum 3 months) with documented development plan.
  - Option C: Separation due to non-fulfillment of performance criteria during probation.
