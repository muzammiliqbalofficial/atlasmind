## 1. HSE Commitment & Safety First Culture
At Atlas Honda Limited, human life and environmental protection take precedence over manufacturing targets. Operating under the banner of **"Safety First, Quality Always"**, our Karachi facility is engineered and certified under **ISO 14001:2015** and **ISO 45001:2018**. 

Zero accidents, zero occupational illnesses, and zero uncontrolled toxic emissions are our foundational operational targets.

## 2. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Matrix by Production Shop

Compliance with the mandatory PPE matrix is enforced at all times. Failure to don required PPE results in immediate removal from the shop floor and disciplinary action.

| Shop / Production Area | Mandatory PPE Requirements | Hazard Controlled |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Press & Stamping Shop** | Steel-toe safety shoes (EN ISO 20345), Level 5 cut-resistant Kevlar gloves, high-grade hearing protection (earmuffs/plugs > 85 dBA), safety glasses with side shields | Heavy sheet metal cutting, pinch points, noise, metal burrs |
| **Welding & Frame Shop** | Auto-darkening welding helmet (DIN 10-12 shade), leather welding jacket & spats, heat-resistant leather gloves, steel-toe boots | Electric arc flash, molten spatter, UV radiation, hot surfaces |
| **Paint & Pre-treatment Shop** | Half-mask respirator with organic vapor/particulate cartridge, lint-free anti-static hooded coverall, nitrile chemical gloves, chemical splash goggles | VOC vapors, paint overspray, acidic phosphating dip tanks |
| **Engine Assembly Line** | Static dissipative (ESD) footwear, lint-free cotton gloves, bump caps, eye protection | Delicate electronic sensors, component sharp edges |
| **Final Assembly & Test Roller** | Safety shoes with oil-resistant soles, bump caps, hearing defenders at dynamometer run-up booth | Running motorcycle rollers, carbon monoxide exhaust, noise |
| **Warehouse & Logistics Bay** | High-visibility fluorescent safety vests (Class 2), steel-toe boots, hard hat when working under high-bay racking | Forklift traffic, falling crate hazards |

## 3. Plant Internal Traffic, Logistics & Speed Limits
- **Speed Limit:** The maximum permissible vehicular speed inside the Karachi Plant perimeter (roads, alleyways, loading docks) is **15 km/h**.
- **Pedestrian Safety:** Pedestrians must walk strictly within the marked yellow safety walkways. Operators must look both ways and sound pedestrian bells at blind intersections.
- **Forklift Operations:** Only licensed and medically certified forklift drivers may operate plant tow-tractors and forklifts. Forks must be carried low (15-20 cm from the ground) when traveling. Forklift drivers must sound horns at all doorway entries and blind corners.
- **Reverse Parking:** All commercial and private vehicles parking in designated plant bays must practice reverse parking (first-move-forward) for rapid emergency evacuation.

## 4. Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) - Zero Energy State Procedure
- No technician, maintenance mechanic, or electrical engineer shall enter any automated robotic welding cell, hydraulic press platen, or conveyor under-pit without executing full **LOTO (Lockout/Tagout)**.
- **Six-Step LOTO Protocol:**
  1. Notify affected line supervisors and operators.
  2. Shut down equipment via normal stopping controls.
  3. Isolate all primary energy sources (electrical disconnect, pneumatic valve venting, hydraulic accumulator bleed).
  4. Apply individual physical padlock and danger tag bearing the technician’s name and phone extension.
  5. Dissipate any stored residual energy (spring tension, hydraulic pressure, elevated counterweights).
  6. Perform the "Test Step" (attempt to turn on power to verify zero energy state) prior to initiating work.

## 5. Hazardous Chemicals & Waste Management
- **Safety Data Sheets (SDS):** Every chemical stored or utilized (cutting oils, degreasers, paint thinners, engine oil lubricants, battery electrolytes) must have a localized SDS displayed in both English and Urdu adjacent to the storage bay.
- **Secondary Containment:** All chemical drums and oil barrels must rest on certified spill pallets with secondary containment capacity of at least 110% of the largest container.
- **Emergency Spill Response:** In the event of a chemical or fuel spill exceeding 20 liters, the area must be cordoned off immediately, spill kit absorbents deployed, and the HSE Response Team dispatched (Plant Emergency Extension: **5555**).

## 6. Incident & Near-Miss Reporting Protocol
- **Near-Miss Intimation:** A near-miss (an incident that had the potential to cause injury or property damage but did not) must be logged on the Plant Safety Incident Form within **two (2) hours** of occurrence.
- **Lost Time Injury (LTI) & Serious Accidents:** Any injury necessitating medical treatment beyond basic first aid must be reported within **thirty (30) minutes** to the Plant Manager, HSE Manager, and Head of Human Resources. An official Root Cause Analysis (RCA) committee will convene within 24 hours.
