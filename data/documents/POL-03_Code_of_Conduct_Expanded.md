## 1. Corporate Philosophy & Foundation
Atlas Honda Limited (ATLH), a premier joint venture between the **Atlas Group (Pakistan)** and **Honda Motor Co. (Japan)**, operates on the foundational principles of integrity, respect for the individual, continuous improvement (Kaizen), and unyielding corporate governance.

Every director, manager, engineer, plant operator, and business associate is bound by the principles established in this Code.

---

## 2. Fundamental Commitments

### 2.1 Obeying the Law
Atlas Honda strictly adheres to all statutory laws, regulations, and industrial standards of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan and applicable international treaties. Ignorance of legal statutes is never accepted as a defense for non-compliance.

### 2.2 Human Capital & Dignity
- **Mutual Trust & Respect:** Every individual is treated with dignity. Harassment, verbal intimidation, bullying, or derogatory conduct based on gender, ethnicity, religion, sect, or physical disability will not be tolerated.
- **Equal Opportunity Employer:** Hiring, remuneration, performance evaluations, and promotions are strictly governed by merit, competence, and organizational performance.
- **No Child or Forced Labour:** Atlas Honda enforces a strict prohibition against child labour (minimum age of 18 for plant employment) and forced or compulsory labour across all direct operations and vendor supply chains.

### 2.3 Customers & Product Safety
- Provide safe, fuel-efficient, durable, and reliable motorcycles that deliver superior value for money.
- Champion road safety through company-sponsored safety riding schools, rider education campaigns, and helmet-wearing promotion across Pakistan.

### 2.4 Shareholders & Corporate Governance
- Maintain transparent, accurate financial accounting compliant with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) and Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) guidelines.
- Protect company assets against waste, theft, loss, and unauthorized commercial exploitation.

---

## 3. Business Integrity & Anti-Corruption (Zero-Tolerance)

### 3.1 Strict Prohibition on Bribery & Kickbacks
- Atlas Honda maintains a **strict zero-tolerance policy** towards bribery, kickbacks, extortion, or improper facilitation payments.
- No employee, contractor, or commercial agent acting on behalf of Atlas Honda shall offer, promise, give, request, or receive any financial or non-financial bribe to influence a business decision or government regulatory action.

### 3.2 Gifts, Entertainment & Hospitality Policy
- **Prohibited Acceptance:** Employees are strictly prohibited from soliciting or accepting personal gifts, loans, luxury travel, or cash equivalents from vendors, suppliers, dealers, or competitors.
- **Modest Promotional Tokens:** Modest business tokens (e.g., promotional calendars, diaries, pens) bearing the vendor’s brand with an aggregate market value **not exceeding PKR 5,000** may be accepted during traditional festive occasions (e.g., Eid or New Year).
- **Mandatory Reporting:** Any gift valued in excess of PKR 5,000 must be reported immediately to the Head of Human Resources and either returned with a polite standard corporate letter or surrendered to the Corporate Pool for internal charity/staff raffle distribution.

---

## 4. Conflicts of Interest
- **Secondary Employment & Commercial Interests:** Employees must not engage in outside employment, commercial consultancies, or operate private businesses that compete with or supply goods/services to Atlas Honda Limited.
- **Transactions with Related Parties:** Any business engagement involving family members, close relatives, or personal commercial affiliations must be formally disclosed in writing to the Company Secretary and approved by the Board Audit Committee.
- **Insider Trading:** Buying or selling Atlas Honda Limited (ATLH) shares while possessing unpublished material price-sensitive information is strictly prohibited and constitutes a criminal offense under SECP regulations.

---

## 5. Media, Public Communication & Social Media
- **Designated Spokespersons:** Only the Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Chief Financial Officer (CFO), and Head of Corporate Communications are authorized to speak to news journalists, television channels, or press outlets regarding Atlas Honda operations.
- **Social Media Conduct:** Employees must not post or share internal technical blueprints, production line photographs (Karachi or Sheikhupura plants), confidential pricing strategies, unreleased motorcycle styling concepts (e.g., CD70/CG125 refreshes), or disparaging statements regarding customers, competitors, or coworkers on social platforms (Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Instagram, X).

---

## 6. Compliance, Monitoring & Protection
- Every department head is accountable for ensuring that all reporting team members receive and acknowledge this Code annually.
- Suspected violations should be reported without hesitation through the dedicated confidential Whistleblower channel. Atlas Honda guarantees confidentiality and strict legal protection against retaliation for good-faith reporting.
