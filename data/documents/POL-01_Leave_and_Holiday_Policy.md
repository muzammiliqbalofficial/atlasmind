## 1. Purpose & Scope
Atlas Honda Limited (AHL) is committed to fostering a balanced, productive, and healthy work environment. This policy governs employee leaves, public holidays, and attendance entitlements in compliance with the Sindh Factories Act, the Industrial and Commercial Employment (Standing Orders) Ordinance, and company HR philosophy.

This policy applies to all permanent, contractual, and probationary staff across the Karachi Plant, corporate offices, and national operational sites.

---

## 2. Categories of Leave

### 2.1 Annual / Privilege Leave (AL)
- **Entitlement:** All permanent employees are entitled to **14 consecutive calendar days** (or working days equivalent) of paid Annual Leave upon completion of twelve (12) months of continuous service.
- **Accrual & Carry Forward:** Employees may accumulate and carry forward up to a maximum of **14 days** of unutilized Annual Leave into the subsequent calendar year. Any balance exceeding 14 days at the close of the financial year (December 31) will either be encashed or lapse based on departmental operational necessity.
- **Application Protocol:** Annual leave requests exceeding three (3) consecutive working days must be submitted through the Atlas Self-Service Portal at least **7 working days** in advance and require approval from the respective Department Head (HOD).

### 2.2 Casual Leave (CL)
- **Entitlement:** Employees are entitled to **10 days** of paid Casual Leave in each calendar year.
- **Purpose:** Intended for unforeseen emergencies, urgent private affairs, or short personal obligations.
- **Limitations:** Casual Leave cannot be taken for more than three (3) consecutive days at any single instance and cannot be combined with Annual Leave without prior written sanction from Head of HR.
- **Non-Accumulation:** Casual Leave cannot be accumulated or carried forward into the next calendar year; unavailed days lapse automatically on December 31.

### 2.3 Sick / Medical Leave (SL)
- **Entitlement:** Permanent employees are entitled to **8 days** of Sick Leave on full pay, or **16 days** on half pay, during each complete year of service.
- **Medical Certification:** Any medical leave extending beyond two (2) consecutive days requires a verifiable Medical Certificate issued by the Atlas Honda Factory Medical Officer (Karachi Plant Clinic) or a registered MBBS doctor recognized by the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PMDC).
- **Hospitalization:** In cases of prolonged illness or hospitalization certified by the company panel hospital, extended medical leave may be granted subject to Medical Board review and Executive Management discretion.

### 2.4 Maternity & Paternity Leave
- **Maternity Leave:** Female employees are entitled to **16 weeks** of paid maternity leave (six weeks preceding and ten weeks following delivery) in accordance with the Sindh Maternity Benefits Act. Applicable for up to three surviving children.
- **Paternity Leave:** Male employees are granted **5 working days** of paid paternity leave upon the birth of a child, claimable within 30 days of childbirth.

### 2.5 Hajj / Religious Pilgrimage Leave
- Employees with at least five (5) years of continuous service at Atlas Honda Limited may apply for special Pilgrimage Leave for performing Hajj.
- Entitlement is granted for up to **40 calendar days** once during the entire employment tenure with the company.

---

## 3. Probationary Period Regulations
- **3.1 Accrual:** Employees currently serving probation (standard 6-month probation) are not entitled to avail Annual Leave until formal confirmation of employment.
- **3.2 Emergency Leaves for Probationers:** Probationers may avail prorated Casual Leave (calculated at 0.83 days per completed month of service) and certified Sick Leave in bona fide medical emergencies, subject to Line Manager and HR endorsement.

---

## 4. Gazetted & Factory Festival Holidays
- Atlas Honda observes all official public holidays declared by the Federal and Sindh Provincial Governments, including:
  - Pakistan Day (March 23)
  - Eid-ul-Fitr (as notified)
  - Labour Day (May 1)
  - Eid-ul-Adha (as notified)
  - Independence Day (August 14)
  - Ashura (9th & 10th Muharram)
  - Eid Milad-un-Nabi (12th Rabi-ul-Awwal)
  - Quaid-e-Azam Day / Christmas (December 25)
- Manufacturing plant shifts working on scheduled gazetted holidays to meet motorcycle assembly delivery quotas will receive compensatory rest days or overtime remuneration in strict compliance with statutory labor laws.

---

## 5. Unexcused Absences & Sanctions
- Any absence without prior approved intimation through the portal or notification to the shift supervisor within 2 hours of shift commencement is marked as **Absent Without Leave (AWOL)**.
- Consecutive absence of three (3) days without notification triggers an automated inquiry notice from the HR Department. Absence exceeding ten (10) consecutive working days without authorization constitutes gross misconduct under the Industrial Standing Orders and may lead to termination of employment.
