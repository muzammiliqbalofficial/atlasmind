## 1. Overview & Information Security Purpose
Information technology assets, enterprise resource planning (SAP S/4HANA), network infrastructure, and electronic databases are essential proprietary assets of Atlas Honda Limited (AHL). This policy establishes acceptable use guidelines to mitigate cybersecurity risks, prevent unauthorized disclosures of technical product blueprints (e.g. Honda CD70/CG125 engineering specs), and define IT support service levels.

---

## 2. Workstation & Hardware Security Standards
- **Screen Lock Automation:** All company-issued desktop computers, CAD engineering workstations, and laptops must be set to automatically lock screens after **5 minutes of inactivity**. Manual locking (`Windows Key + L`) is mandatory when leaving desks.
- **Removable Media & USB Storage:** To safeguard against industrial espionage, ransomware, and intellectual property theft, unauthorized USB flash drives and portable hard drives are restricted by Group Policy. Only IT-encrypted, approved media may be utilized for plant data collection.
- **Asset Care:** Laptops assigned to staff must be securely stored. Theft or damage resulting from gross negligence must be reported to the IT Department and Plant Security within 2 hours.

---

## 3. Network, Email & Internet Use
- **Corporate Email (@atlashonda.com.pk):** The company email system is provided exclusively for official business communications. Automatic forwarding of internal emails to personal mailboxes (e.g., Gmail, Yahoo) is strictly prohibited and blocked by Mail Gateways.
- **Prohibited Online Activities:**
  - Engaging in cryptocurrency mining, peer-to-peer (P2P) file sharing, torrenting, or streaming non-business media during office hours.
  - Accessing gambling, illicit, or politically controversial online material via company LAN, Wi-Fi, or VPN connections.
  - Using unauthorized remote desktop utilities (e.g., TeamViewer, AnyDesk) without explicit written authorization from IT Infrastructure Security.

---

## 4. ERP (SAP S/4HANA) & Application Credentials
- **Credential Integrity:** User credentials (usernames and passwords) are personal and non-transferable. Sharing SAP credentials, master passwords, or supervisory override codes with subordinates or peers is classified as **Gross Misconduct**.
- **Password Complexity:** Passwords must be at least 12 characters long, containing uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters, and must be updated every **90 days**.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** SAP access privileges are allocated strictly according to job roles (Least Privilege Principle). Privilege elevation requires approval from the Department Head and Internal Audit.

---

## 5. Software Licensing & Intellectual Property
- Atlas Honda maintains zero tolerance for unauthorized or pirated software. 
- Only software procured and installed by the AHL IT Department (licensed Microsoft Windows, Office 365, Dassault CATIA, AutoCAD, SAP GUI) may be installed.
- The IT department conducts automated network audits monthly; unapproved executables will be removed remotely without notice.

---

## 6. IT Service Desk Support & Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

The IT Service Desk operates from the Karachi Head Office and coordinates support nationwide. Employees can log support tickets via the **Atlas IT Helpdesk Portal** or contact internal extension **4321** / email `itsupport@atlashonda.com.pk`.

| Severity Level | Definition & Criteria | Target Response Time | Target Resolution SLA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Severity 1 (Critical)** | Entire plant production line stoppage, core SAP ERP outage, primary network backbone down at F-36 S.I.T.E. | 15 Minutes | Within 1 Hour |
| **Severity 2 (High)** | Department-wide interruption (e.g., Finance payroll processing, Supply Chain logistics bay system), Senior Executive VIP issue | 30 Minutes | Within 4 Hours |
| **Severity 3 (Medium)** | Individual workstation malfunction, localized printer error, single SAP transaction bug | 1 Hour | Within 24 Business Hours |
| **Severity 4 (Low)** | General hardware requests, software upgrades, routine password resets, peripheral replenishment | 4 Hours | Within 48 Business Hours |
