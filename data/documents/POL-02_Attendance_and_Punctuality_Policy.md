## 1. Objective & Operational Context
Manufacturing high-precision motorcycles (including the iconic CD70, CG125, and Pridor series) requires synchronized shop-floor production lines. Punctuality and consistent attendance are foundational to maintaining Atlas Honda's operational cadence and meeting daily assembly quotas. This policy defines shift timings, biometric recording rules, grace intervals, and disciplinary procedures for attendance non-compliance.

---

## 2. Shift Schedules & Working Hours

### 2.1 Karachi Plant Manufacturing Shifts
The Karachi plant operates on a 24/6 rotational shift basis with designated shifts as follows:
- **Shift A (Morning Shift):** 07:00 HRS to 15:30 HRS (inclusive of a 30-minute lunch/tea interval).
- **Shift B (Evening Shift):** 15:30 HRS to 00:00 HRS (inclusive of a 30-minute dinner/tea interval).
- **Shift C (Night Shift):** 00:00 HRS to 07:30 HRS (inclusive of a 30-minute refreshment interval).
- *Shift Handover Protocol:* Shift supervisors and incoming operators must arrive **10 minutes prior** to official shift commencement to ensure seamless hand-off of machine line logs and safety interlock checklists.

### 2.2 Corporate & Administrative Office Hours
- **Standard Corporate Hours:** 08:30 HRS to 17:15 HRS (Monday through Friday).
- **Lunch & Prayer Recess:** 13:00 HRS to 14:00 HRS (Friday congregational prayers: 12:45 HRS to 14:15 HRS).

---

## 3. Biometric Time Recording Protocols
- **Mandatory Punching:** All employees, regardless of grade or designation, must register their physical presence using the biometric smart card and facial recognition turnstiles at Plant Gate 1, Gate 2, or Corporate Lobby entrances.
- **Punch In / Punch Out:** Employees must log both their entry punch upon arrival and exit punch upon shift conclusion. Failure to punch out will be treated as an incomplete record requiring Line Manager manual attestation.
- **Proxy Punching:** Attempting to record attendance on behalf of another employee, or tampering with biometric scanners, constitutes **Major Misconduct** and warrants immediate disciplinary action under the Disciplinary Policy.

---

## 4. Punctuality, Grace Periods & Deductions

### 4.1 Grace Period
- A permissible grace period of **ten (10) minutes** from official shift commencement is extended to accommodate unexpected transit or traffic delays.
- Arrival within the 10-minute grace window is permitted up to a maximum of **three (3) times per calendar month** without salary penalty.

### 4.2 Late Arrival Penalties
- **4th Late Occurrence:** A fourth (4th) late arrival within a single calendar month results in the automated deduction of **half-day (0.5) Casual Leave** or equivalent pro-rata basic wage deduction if casual leave balance is zero.
- **Subsequent Late Arrivals:** Any additional late arrivals (5th and beyond) trigger direct consultation with the Department Head and may result in an official Written Caution Notice recorded in the employee's HR dossier.
- **Habitual Tardy Pattern:** Arrival beyond 30 minutes past shift start without prior intimation is classified as "Half-Day Absence" unless expressly regularized by the Shift In-Charge.

---

## 5. Overtime Guidelines (Plant Technicians & Operators)
- Overtime (OT) work must be strictly pre-authorized by the Production Planning & Control (PPC) Department and approved by the Shop Manager.
- In compliance with the Sindh Factories Act, eligible non-management factory staff performing authorized work beyond normal daily shift hours will receive overtime compensation at **double the ordinary rate of basic pay (2.0x)**.
- For safety reasons, no plant technician is permitted to work consecutive double shifts exceeding 16 continuous hours under any circumstances.

---

## 6. Absence Reporting Protocol
- **Advance Intimation:** Employees unable to report for their scheduled shift due to sudden emergency or sickness must inform their direct Line Supervisor via telephone, SMS, or WhatsApp group at least **one (1) hour prior** to shift start.
- **Duty Resumption:** Upon returning from sick leave or emergency absence, the employee must report to the HR Time Office with relevant documentation prior to resuming work on the line.
